Hobbies

Things I enjoy doing in my free time.

Rowing

Early-morning rows clear my head and give me a focused, energized start to the day. I loved being a coxswain at a competitive level for 5 years and hope to return to the sport one day.

Golf

Golf is where patience and precision come to test me. I enjoy the challenge of strategizing each shot and love walking alongside beautiful courses. I enjoy going with my friends and family, but don't mind hitting balls alone. I recently worked as a cocktail server for LIV golf's Virginia tournament and visited the Memorial tournament in Ohio as a guest. Hopefully one day I can watch the Masters in person!

Travelling

I love to take in the culture and history of new places. I've made it a habit to test out the ice cream of each destination! I enjoy recharging and taking in beautiful scenery. A fresh perspective and location fuels my creativity!

Writing

Writing helps me process ideas and understand the world around me. It also allows me to connect with the contemplative spirit inside myself. My favorite mediums are short fiction, novels, and poetry. I'm hoping to write a dramatic screenplay soon or perhaps publish a dystopian novel soon, but I'm a pretty busy lady!

Read my first book Check out my Substack for recent writes

Coffee walks

Long walks and hikes with a great iced coffee or energy drink are my favorite pastime. It's perfect for catching up with friends, waking up, and moving your body. Recently, I've been loving an iced salted caramel latte at Starnut, a local gourmet coffee spot in my hometown.

Community service

I volunteer regularly, especially through my service fraternity at Georgetown. We serve food to people experiencing homelessness at a local church, make cards for oncology patients, and clean up local parks, contributing a minimum of 30 service hours per semester. Community work grounds me and connects me with purpose and people.
